    Showcase Social Media

    No. 1 DIY brand thanks to influencers

    New on the German market – without a German website or social media channels and only available in brick-and-mortar stores – we introduced the American paint manufacturer Rust-Oleum with its key product chalk paint to the DIY-savvy target group in Germany. To achieve this, we developed a sophisticated social media and influencer strategy aimed at the young, female DIY scene. With DIY bloggers, various micro-influencers, targeted social ad placement and retargeting, we generated over 50,000 followers and 350% sales growth across all channels. Rust-Oleum thus became Germany’s No. 1 brand in its segment.

    Showcase Public Relations

    PR event in online stream

    International participants, interactive live broadcast: This was the online press conference for the renowned fittings manufacturer HANSA. The Oras Group company has more than a hundred years of experience in the manufacture of high-quality and innovative sanitary products. In order to communicate important news to journalists during the Corona crisis, BLUE MOON designed a live online press conference, including moderation, website and a time-synchronized question box. A complete success: a total of 60 journalists took part in the live event, resulting in more than 90 press releases.

    Showcase Online Marketing

    Online leads for the future of heating

    How much does a new heat pump cost? At mehrwaerme.de, homeowners can calculate their contracting rate within a minute. Our customer mehrwärme offers full-service heating contracting for private property owners. To reach this target group, BLUE MOON developed the website mehrwaerme.de: It has a quotation calculator that easily provides users with a meaningful calculation for a heating system to suit their individual property. Our customer gains valuable leads in this way – over 1,000 a year. And best of all: thanks to a sophisticated SEO strategy, no ad placement is necessary!

    Showcase Branding

    Footballers stop burglars

    Teamwork, strategy and responsiveness – our brand campaign visualizes the strengths of the security service provider Protection One. Our client, an innovative provider of remote surveillance, is characterized by its split-second response to break-ins. To visualize this speed of action, real football players were dressed in branded outfits. Action-packed videos and photos were created over two nights. In a cross-media campaign, these motifs gave Protection One a unique, unmissable appearance. BLUE MOON was responsible for the creative concept, shooting and campaign management.
